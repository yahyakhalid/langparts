Welcome, international students

We are glad you chose to study with us. This page explains how to register, where to find housing and how to reach the student office.

For our Arabic-speaking students: مرحباً بكم في الجامعة، نتمنى لكم سنة دراسية موفقة.

أهلاً وسهلاً بكم في الحرم الجامعي is the greeting you will see at the main gate.

The campus is named after the poet المتنبي, and the library holds a copy of ألف ليلة وليلة.
